## Short public summary

Delta Intelligence tracks factory openings and expansions in Malaysia. Analysts can search by company, state, milestone and publication date, then inspect the exact primary-source excerpt and a follow-up question for rooftop solar due diligence. Delta buys search through Telegraph and checks whether the provider cited the exact article. In two Base Sepolia trials, it turned the Eppendorf groundbreaking source into a dated screening result and withheld an MKS claim when the provider returned only a press index. Each call cost 0.01 faucet USDC.

## Commercial questions

1. **Who is it for?** Commercial rooftop solar teams and analysts tracking industrial capacity. The workflow fits a recurring search for construction and expansion announcements.
2. **What does it improve?** It links each candidate to its actual release and prevents common timeline, alias and existing-installation mistakes. On 13 authored local cases, dated evidence matching produced every expected report. Literal matching hit five cases. These cases exercise behaviour under controlled inputs.
3. **What works today?** A local browser app, nine sites from ten live-checked releases, and a bounded testnet purchase that can populate an evidence-backed result. Two x402 purchases settled on Base Sepolia. Their transfers and signal hashes were independently verified.
4. **Why adopt it?** Sales teams can decide which sites to investigate before spending time on site assessments. Broader source coverage, customer workflow trials and operating-cost data are still open.
5. **How can someone try it?** Run the repository with Node.js, try a new search and open the Telegraph evidence panel. The README also shows how to request unsigned quotes and reproduce a faucet-funded purchase. Maintainer identity follows the registration profile.

## Demo and reviewer evidence

1. Start the desk and open the Eppendorf historical preset. The as-of date hides the later groundbreaking and shows the July lease release.
2. Set the Through date to 25 November and run the search again. Compare the 12 November groundbreaking to the July lease and inspect the primary source.
3. Expand **Review Telegraph testnet purchases**. Open the Eppendorf transfer and signal. The paid provider returned the exact article and the project independently checked its publication excerpt against the snapshot.
4. Open the MKS result in the purchase viewer. The miner cited only the press index. The project withheld the report and still retained the real receipt.
5. Search Honda or an unknown company. The first shows **Hold** for existing solar, and the second returns **Evidence unavailable**. Download the case result as JSON.
